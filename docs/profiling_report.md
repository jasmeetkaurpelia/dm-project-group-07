## Key findings

- **Enough data, clean structure.** 10,839 rows for Heidelberg (4,684) and Mannheim (6,155) from 30 counter sites over all 365 days of 2025. There are no duplicates, no negative counts and no bad dates, and IN + OUT = ALL in every row that has direction data.
- **111 site-days are missing (coverage 98.99 %), at 7 sites.** 76 of them are because 3 counters only start reporting between 24 and 29 January (Jungbuschbrücke, Schlierbacher Landstraße, Ziegelhäuser Landstraße). The other 35 are gaps during the year, most at Schlosspark Lindenhof (16) and Lindenhofüberführung (9).
- **97 zero-count days are mostly sensor outages, not real zero traffic.** 59 are at B38 Ri. IN and 29 at Liebermannstraße, a site that averages 4,476 bikes a day. Used as 0, they would teach an AI model wrong patterns, so they must be flagged and set to NULL.
- **Hidden missing values and no data types.** All 17 columns arrive as text (type conformity 0 %). 366 rows have the text `na` instead of in/out counts; they only have a total, under `channels_unknown`. Site names are inconsistent (typo "Thedor-Heuss-Brücke", trailing dots like "Fernmeldeturm.", "Feudenheimstr." vs "Feudenheimerstr."), and the timezone label says Europe/Paris.
- **Weather is in good shape.** DWD Mannheim has all 365 days of 2025 with full temperature and rain. In the historical file, fewer than 1 % of values are `-999`, only for wind, cloud, pressure and humidity. The historical and recent files overlap on 245 days with identical values, so keeping the historical file loses nothing.
